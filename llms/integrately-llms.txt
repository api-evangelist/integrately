# Integrately

> Integrately is a one-click workflow automation platform (iPaaS) operated by CompanyHub It
> Solutions Private Limited. It connects 1,100+ SaaS applications through a catalog of ready-made
> automation templates, positioning itself as a lower-cost, no-code alternative to Zapier and Make.
> IMPORTANT FOR AGENTS: Integrately publishes NO developer API. There is no OpenAPI, no GraphQL
> endpoint, no MCP server, no A2A agent card, no SDK and no API key programme. You cannot drive
> Integrately programmatically. What Integrately offers a builder is (a) an inbound webhook URL
> that starts an automation, (b) an outbound HTTP step that calls your API, and (c) an embeddable
> JavaScript widget for putting Integrately's automation catalog inside your own product.

## What Integrately is

- Product: a no-code workflow automation platform. A user picks two or more apps, picks a
  ready-made automation ("1-click automation") or builds one, maps fields, and turns it on.
- Metered unit: the "task". Each successful action, condition/branch, Modify Data and Search
  execution consumes one task. Triggers are free and failed actions are not counted.
- Scale observed 2026-09-13 from Integrately's own public catalog endpoints: 1,100 connectable
  apps across 63 categories, 295 of them OAuth-based, 31 flagged premium, and 340,365 stored
  automation templates.
- Legal entity: CompanyHub It Solutions Private Limited.

## How a developer actually integrates with Integrately

There are exactly three documented routes, and none of them is an API Integrately publishes.

1. Inbound webhook trigger. Integrately mints a unique URL per automation. POST anything to it and
   the automation runs. The payload schema is whatever you send — Integrately derives mappable
   fields from a sample you supply during setup. App: "Webhook / API Integration" (slug
   webhook-api), trigger "Operation is performed". Docs:
   https://integrately.com/docs#connect-webhook
2. Outbound HTTP action. An automation step named "Send data to a URL" that calls your endpoint.
   Same app.
3. Embeddable widget. A script tag that renders Integrately's automation catalog inside your own
   site or SaaS product, optionally filtered to your app so your users only see automations
   involving you:
     <script src="https://embed.integrately.com/embed?limit=10&custom=true"></script>
     <div id="integrately-embed"></div>
   Query parameters: apps, limit, custom, strict. The distribution is unpinned — there is no
   version in the URL, so you cannot tell which build you are loading.
   Docs: https://integrately.com/embed

## Plans and limits (published, 2026-09-13)

Prices in USD. Two bases are published per paid tier: billed monthly, and a lower
monthly-equivalent when billed annually. "Update time" is the polling interval for triggers that
are not webhook-delivered.

- Free — $0. 100 tasks/month, 15-minute update time, 5 automations, single-step only.
- Starter — $29.99/mo, or $19.99/mo billed annually. 2,000 tasks/month, 5-minute update time,
  20 automations, 3 premium apps, 1 user.
- Professional — $49/mo, or $39/mo billed annually. 10,000 tasks/month, 2-minute update time,
  unlimited automations and premium apps, 1 user.
- Growth — $124/mo, or $99/mo billed annually. 30,000 tasks/month, 2-minute update time,
  unlimited everything, dedicated account manager.
- Business — $299/mo, or $239/mo billed annually ($2,868/year). 150,000 tasks/month, 2-minute
  update time, unlimited users, folder permissions, dedicated account manager.

A 14-day free trial is advertised. No overage pricing is published: when the task allowance runs
out, actions stop and records queue inside Integrately until you upgrade, resumable from the
History page. No enterprise tier is published.

## Operational semantics worth knowing

- AutoRetry: records that failed in the previous 24 hours are retried automatically when you fix a
  failing automation and set it live again. When a connected app is unreachable, Integrately keeps
  trying for roughly 10.5 hours. This is forward recovery, not reversal.
- No reversal path. Integrately documents no undo, rollback or compensation for a task that has
  already written into a connected app. Treat every automation as irreversible once it fires.
- No idempotency mechanism and no webhook signature verification are documented. If you POST to an
  Integrately webhook URL, carry your own unique key in the payload and rely on the destination
  app's deduplication.
- Compliance: the privacy policy (last updated 12 Sept 2024) is written against GDPR and adopts
  the Article 4 Controller/Processor definitions. No SOC 2, ISO 27001 or HIPAA claim exists and
  there is no trust centre.
